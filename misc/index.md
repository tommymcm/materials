Here are some things that I have built and collected for my own personal enjoyment.

## 🃏 [Games](/games)

I like playing solitaire card games. I am slowly building online versions of each one so that I can play when I don't have a deck of cards on hand. All of these games are [open-source](https://github.com/tommymcm/solitaire).

***


## 🍲 [Recipes](/misc/recipes)

I love cooking, but rarely do it from a recipe. Whenever I nail a difficult dish I *try* to write it down to use it later. This is most true for baking, since it is less forgiving. I have also collected some recipes from my Mom, Grandma, and Nonna to preserve and share.

***

## 📸 [Photography](/misc/photos)

I travel around the states whenever I can, and try to take time to go and explore other countries whenever I'm there for a conference. While I am not a great photographer, and only take photos with my phone, I have collected some good ones over the years.
